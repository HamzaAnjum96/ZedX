# Product marketing context

Read by the `copywriting` and `copy-editing` skills before they write or edit
anything for this site.

## Who is writing

The site is run by an independent promotional partner of ZedX. We do not develop, own or support ZedX:
Swiftpro Corporation Ltd (London) does. Say so plainly near the top of every page and in the footer.

- **Never** write "our software", "our platform" or "we built".
- **"We"** means the promotional partner. Use it only where the site explains who runs it.
- **No invented names:** no business name or logo for the partner is verified yet, so don't invent one.

## What we promote

- **Group WorkStreams.** Continuous, operational work, without the overhead of a project plan or sprints. Each function of an organisation (complaints, catering, HR requests, finance queries) gets its own workstream, and its tasks sit on a Kanban board with stages set for that workstream. A workstream can also be a simple list or a timeline, and dashboards show workload, progress and bottlenecks. Swiftpro has had its own Group WorkStreams page since October 2026.
- **Agile Sprints.** Planned Scrum delivery: epics and user stories in a prioritised backlog, story points and team capacity, sprints on a configurable board, stand-ups and sprint reviews, releases across sprints, and metrics for completion, velocity and carried-over work.
- **Project Portfolios** (supporting role only). Where leaders follow delivery through structured updates that project managers submit at agreed intervals, such as monthly or quarterly. It is not a real-time feed of tasks.

## Audience

- **Who:** operations teams, delivery managers and the people who approve software, in UK public sector, education and business organisations that are looking at ZedX.
- **What they need to know:**
  - which app fits which kind of work;
  - what the screens look like;
  - how sign-in, hosting and licensing work;
  - how to get a demo.

## Facts and proof

- **Allowed claims:** only those in `replica/claims.md`, and only with their conditions intact.
  - Editions: Home, Professional, Group, Corporate and Enterprise. SaaS editions are licensed per user; Enterprise has unlimited users and can run in the customer's own Microsoft Azure.
  - Integration is described only for Enterprise deployments.
  - Claims Swiftpro withdrew in October 2026 (guest logins, ZedX's own sign-in, licence blocks, own-server hosting, Swiftpro Digital) stay off the site.
- **Evidence:** real screens from the ZedX demo environment, with its sample college data. Describe only what is visible.
- **Nothing invented:** no customers, testimonials, numbers, outcomes, prices or awards. If a claim needs proof we don't have, mark it `[NEED: ...]` and leave it out of the page.

## The action

- **Main action:** "Request a demo". It opens the contact form on zedxapps.com, and the message goes to Swiftpro. Their form says they will get back to you.
- **Never imply** an instant booking or a calendar.
- **Secondary actions:** "Try the demo yourself" (the public ZedX demo at demo.zedx.net, no registration), the PDF brochure and the ZedX customer login. Swiftpro also offers a free trial with no credit card; there is no public sign-up link, so tell people to ask Swiftpro.

## Voice

- **Plain British English:** organisation, prioritised, licence (noun).
- **Tone:** professional and friendly, like a knowledgeable colleague, not an ad.
- **Short sentences, active verbs.** Name the product and the action. Prefer the words the products use themselves: workstream, stage, work item, backlog, sprint, story points.
- **Banned:**
  - the AI tells in `.claude/skills/copywriting/references/ai-tells.md`: "not X but Y" reveals, negation lists, trailing comma pile-ons, self-answered questions, colon reveals, em dashes in headings and short copy;
  - stock phrases, and filler such as unlock, seamless, all-in-one and revolutionise;
  - competitor names.
- **No eyebrow labels or kickers above headings.** Headings carry the product name and make a claim.
