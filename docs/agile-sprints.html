ZedX app for Scrum delivery

Agile Sprints

Sprint and release management for delivery teams working in Scrum: epics and stories in a prioritised backlog, planned into sprints and tracked on a sprint board.

Request a demo Compare with Group WorkStreams

Real screens from the ZedX demo environment, showing its sample college data.

Plan from a prioritised backlog

Work is organised into epics and user stories in a prioritised product backlog. Stories are estimated in story points and selected into sprints during planning, so scope, capacity and sprint goals are visible before work begins.

In the demo, the backlog has three stages of its own: Ideas & Intake, Refinement Needed and Ready for Sprint.

See epics over time

Epics can be laid out on a timeline, week by week, so a team can see how the larger pieces of work overlap across sprints. Releases can also span one or more sprints, with stories assigned to planned release versions.

Run the sprint, then learn from it

Sprint board
Each sprint has a visual board with configurable workflow stages, showing work in progress, completed items and blocked work.
Stand-ups and reviews
Daily stand-ups and sprint reviews are supported, with online automated stand-up reporting. Sprint progress is tracked in real time.
Metrics
Sprint completion, velocity and carried-over work, with trends across sprints to improve estimation. The official page says the metrics are there to inform teams and encourage learning, not to enforce control.
Access
Role-based permissions control what each person can do.
