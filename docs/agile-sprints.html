Disciplined sprints, without the admin.

Plan

A backlog the whole team can plan from

Organise work into epics and user stories in a prioritised product backlog. Estimate stories in points, then select them into the sprint against the team's capacity, so scope and the sprint goal are clear before work begins.

  • Epics and user stories, in priority order
  • Story points and team capacity side by side
  • Priorities can change as requirements evolve

Run

Stand-ups that start from the facts

Each sprint runs on a configurable board that shows work in progress, finished work and anything blocked. Stand-up reporting is produced automatically, so the conversation starts from the facts rather than a round of status updates.

  • Board stages configured to match your workflow
  • Blocked work stands out
  • Sprint progress tracked in real time
  • Support for daily stand-ups and sprint reviews

Try it in the sample sprint

Review

Velocity that informs rather than judges

See sprint completion, velocity and carried-over work for every sprint, and the trend across sprints. Teams use it to sharpen their estimates and understand how they deliver.

  • Completion and velocity per sprint
  • Carried-over work made visible
  • Trends across many sprints

Release

Releases planned across sprints

Plan releases that span one or more sprints, and assign each story to the version it will ship in, so everyone can see what is coming and when.

  • Releases across one or more sprints
  • Stories assigned to planned versions
  • Epics laid out on a timeline

How it thinks about metrics

Metrics are there to help teams learn and improve, not to police them.

Learning, not pressure

Completion, velocity and carried-over work are there to improve estimates and delivery, not to rank people.

The right access

Role-based permissions control who can see and change what.

No unnecessary process

Built around sprint execution, team capacity and flow, without extra governance overhead.

Project Portfolios
