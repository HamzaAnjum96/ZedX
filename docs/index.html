Why teams struggle

Most work tools are either too heavy or too light

And the people who need to know how things are going still end up chasing for updates.

Too heavy

Developer-grade trackers can need specialists to configure and maintain. Every new field, workflow or permission becomes a small project, and simple work gets buried.

Too light

Simple card boards are quick to start, then run out of road when you need permissions, structure across teams, or reporting someone can rely on.

Watched, not helped

When a tool becomes a way to police people, teams spend their time feeding it, and leaders still chase status by email.

ZedX gives each kind of work the board it needs: Kanban workflows for operational teams, Scrum for delivery teams. Both sit on one platform, and leaders get structured summaries instead of task-level detail.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can we use just one of the apps?

Yes. Each ZedX app can be licensed on its own, and you can add the other later. Both share the same login and interface, so adding one does not mean learning a new system.

How do the two apps work together?

Teams with a mixed delivery model use Agile Sprints for their Scrum work and Group WorkStreams for operational and ad-hoc work, which keeps sprints focused. Either app can be linked to Project Portfolios for oversight.

Can people outside our organisation take part?

Yes. External users can sign in with their own Microsoft Entra ID or be given a guest login, and permissions control what they can see and do.

Where is our data held?

ZedX runs on Microsoft Azure. Swiftpro can host it for you, with data stored and processed in the UK, or it can run in your own Azure subscription.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The apps are fully usable in a mobile browser, so there is nothing to install.

Can it match the way we already work?

Each sector edition comes preconfigured, and the apps can then be configured further to fit your organisation's processes.

Can we bring our existing lists across?

You can import data from CSV, and export to Excel or PDF at any time.
