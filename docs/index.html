Group WorkStreams takes everyday work out of spreadsheets

Complaints, catering faults, HR requests and finance queries can each become a workstream, with its own Kanban board, stages and team. Everyone works from one board instead of a spreadsheet, and each item shows who has it and how urgent it is.

It suits operational, administrative and service teams whose work arrives every day, outside any project plan or sprint. A dashboard shows managers where work is building up, so they don’t have to ask each person for an update.

See how Group WorkStreams works

Agile Sprints takes Scrum teams from backlog to release

Epics and user stories wait in a prioritised backlog. At sprint planning, the team estimates each story in story points and takes on only what its capacity allows.

During the sprint, the board shows what is in progress, finished or blocked, and stand-up reporting is automated. At the end, the team reviews its velocity and any work carried over, then formally closes the sprint. Stories are assigned to planned releases, which can span several sprints.

Leaders follow progress in Project Portfolios

The boards belong to the teams doing the work. Project Portfolios, a third ZedX app, is for the people who oversee that work: PMOs, programme managers and senior leadership teams.

Project managers send it a structured update at agreed intervals, such as monthly or quarterly. Each update rates delivery confidence red, amber or green and adds a progress note, achievements, blockers, risks, dependencies and any support needed. Reminders go out automatically when updates are due, so less time goes on chasing them.

Dashboards show delivery health across portfolios, programmes and projects. Trends show whether confidence is improving, stable or getting worse.

A project can link its workstreams to Group WorkStreams or Agile Sprints. Project Portfolios then sees their progress as a summary in each update. The individual tasks stay on the team’s board.

Project Portfolios also covers vision and objectives, a portfolio Gantt chart, financial reporting, Excel export and benefits tracking after delivery. Read about Project Portfolios on zedxapps.com
