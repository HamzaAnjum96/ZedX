Group WorkStreams

Continuous work, organised by function

Set up a workstream for each function, such as complaints, catering, HR requests or finance queries. Its tasks sit on a Kanban board, with stages that fit that kind of work.

Suits
Operational teams handling a steady flow of requests, cases and issues: continuous-flow, ad-hoc and non-Scrum work.
How work is organised
Each workstream has its own board and stages, set from New Stage and Edit Stage. In the demo, the complaints log runs through five stages; catering issues need three.
What teams can see
Each workstream’s portfolio, active epics and active tasks, and an Insights dashboard of work items per workstream, split into critical and non-critical.
Alongside other apps
Run it next to Agile Sprints where teams mix delivery models. Project workstreams in Project Portfolios can link to it, with progress summarised through structured project updates.

Agile Sprints

Planned delivery, sprint by sprint

For teams working in Scrum. Epics and user stories sit in a prioritised backlog, are estimated in story points and are planned into sprints against the team’s capacity.

Suits
Delivery teams working in Scrum or sprint-based Agile, where work is planned before it starts.
The sprint
A sprint board with configurable workflow stages shows work in progress, completed and blocked work. Daily stand-ups and sprint reviews are supported, with online automated stand-up reporting.
Releases and metrics
Releases span one or more sprints, with stories assigned to planned versions. Metrics cover sprint completion, velocity and carried-over work, with trends across sprints.
Alongside other apps
Use it on its own, or link it to Project Portfolios: sprint progress is summarised through periodic updates, without task-level detail.

What leadership sees

Leaders follow delivery in a third ZedX app, Project Portfolios. Project managers submit structured updates at agreed intervals, such as monthly or quarterly: delivery confidence as a RAG status, narrative progress, achievements, blockers, risks, dependencies and support required. Dashboards show delivery health across portfolios, programmes and projects. Progress from either app arrives through these periodic updates, not as task-level detail.

What adoption involves

Sign-in and access
Single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID or a ZedX ID; external users with their own Entra ID or a guest login; access to individual apps or modules, with hierarchical permissions.
Hosting
Per-user and per-block licences include hosting. Unlimited-user licences run on your own server or Azure subscription.
Integration
Where ZedX runs on your own servers or Azure, Swiftpro Digital can integrate it with Oracle, SAP ERP or, for education, Tribal, as a separate service.
Licensing
Per app per user; per app in blocks of 10 or 100 users; per app for unlimited users; or every current and future app, unlimited. Swiftpro quotes for each.
