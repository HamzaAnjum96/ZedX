Privacy policy

This site does not collect your personal data. Below is what its host, GitHub, logs and where your details go if you request a demo.

Last updated 4 October 2026

Who is responsible

This site is run by an independent promoter of ZedX, not by Swiftpro Corporation Ltd.

To contact the site owner, open an issue in the site’s repository at github.com/HamzaAnjum96/ZedX/issues. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.

What this site collects

This site does not collect personal data itself. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking code and has no forms.

The fonts, icons, screenshots and brochure are all served from this site, so your browser does not contact other services while you read it.

What GitHub logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service from GitHub, Inc. GitHub logs and stores the IP address of everyone who visits a GitHub Pages site, for security purposes. GitHub decides how that information is used and kept, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

When you leave this site

The “Request a demo” buttons open the contact form on zedxapps.com, which Swiftpro Corporation Ltd runs. Anything you send through that form goes to Swiftpro and is covered by Swiftpro’s Privacy Policy.

The “Log in to ZedX” links go to zedx.net, the ZedX customer login.

Your rights

Because this site holds no personal data about you, it has nothing to give you a copy of or to delete. For GitHub’s visitor logs, contact GitHub. For anything you sent through the ZedX contact form, contact Swiftpro.

If you are in the UK and unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

If this site starts to collect data, for example through analytics or a contact form, this policy will be updated first. The date at the top shows when it last changed. The legal notice explains who runs the site and how far you can rely on it.

About this site

An independent promotional website for two ZedX apps. Swiftpro Corporation Ltd does not run this site. ZedX, ZedX Apps, Group WorkStreams, Agile Sprints and Project Portfolios are products of Swiftpro Corporation Ltd, London.
