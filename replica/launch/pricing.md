Source: G-Cloud 14 pricing document, "Last Updated on 7th May 2024",

| Agile Sprints | £150 | £4,995 | £495 | £150 |
| Corporate Workflows (probably today's Group WorkStreams: a guess) | £150 | £4,995 | £495 | £150 |
| Entire platform, all applications | n/a | £30,000 | £2,500 | n/a |

Notes from the same document: buyers can choose 10-user packs or a
per-organisation licence, separately for each app; a per-organisation licence
hosted in the customer's own Azure subscription includes apps added to the
platform later. The G-Cloud listing (same service ID) also mentioned an
education discount and a 4-week trial account on request.

Implied entry point in 2024 for one app on Swiftpro hosting: £150 licence +
£150 hosting per 10 users = **£300 per 10 users per month**, which matches the
listing's headline "£300 to £500 a unit a month".

The current ZedX site (https://www.zedxapps.com/Platform/index.html) lists
the licensing *options* (per user, packs of 10 or 100 users, unlimited per
app on own server or Azure, everything unlimited on own server or Azure) but
no prices.

## What reviewers said about price

From `feedback.md` (filtered sample): 18 reviews across 3 sources mention
price, per-seat costs or paywalls; the clearest complaint is paying extra
for add-ons on top of a subscription. Thin evidence, but consistent with
per-seat pricing being a known pain in the category.

## Recommendation

1. **No prices on the brochure.** The only public ZedX figures are G-Cloud 14
   prices from May 2024. They may have changed, and a brochure that quotes an
   old price creates a problem at the first sales call.
2. **Show the licensing options instead** (they are on the current site) and
   make "Ask for a quote" the call to action. Done on index.html#licensing.
3. **If Swiftpro confirms current prices**, the strongest honest line is the
   unlimited-users option, which answers the per-seat complaint directly:
   "One app, unlimited users, on your own Azure subscription: from £X a
   month." Add it to the licensing cards with the date.
4. Billing fixes from the replica-launch checklist (one-click cancel, renewal
   emails, no surprise per-seat jumps) are product and contract questions for
   Swiftpro, not brochure copy.
